# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-04

- Durable LangGraph incident workflow with strict PostgreSQL checkpoints.
- Real MCP operational evidence and checkout rollback over two synthetic services.
- Structured OpenRouter investigation bounded to two assessments/evidence rounds.
- Exact persisted action approval, guarded execution, and independent recovery verification.
- FastAPI control plane with transient SSE and later durable approval/resume.
- Six-scenario DEV/HOLDOUT evaluator with retained scripted/live candidate results.
- Verified dependency constraints and CI for quality, real integration, and scripted evaluation.
- MIT license and release documentation.

See [known limitations](README.md#known-limitations): v1 is a local/demo project,
not a production SRE platform or a broad reliability benchmark.
